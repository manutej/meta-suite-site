meta-research

A rigorous, cross-verified, source-cited brief — with explicit confidence and reusable knowledge atoms.

// what it does

A confident-sounding paragraph is not research. meta-research gathers independent sources, cross-checks them against each other, and states how sure it is — with the citation attached to every claim, not a bibliography dumped at the end.

Where sources disagree, it says so instead of picking a winner. And it breaks findings into reusable knowledge atoms — small, cited facts you can carry into the next question.

// the brief
cross-verified

Claims checked against independent sources, not one confident page.

confidence, stated

Every finding says how sure — and flags where sources conflict.

knowledge atoms

Small, cited facts you can reuse in the next question.

Grounding

The ideas here aren't invented — the skill generalizes established practice into one reusable, task-agnostic move. Below: the prior art it builds on, and the in-repo contract it actually runs on.

Prior art
Triangulation — corroborate every load-bearing claim across independent sources; flag anything that rests on one.
GRADE — grading the certainty of evidence (high / moderate / low) — report confidence per conclusion; never launder inference as fact.
Disconfirming evidence — actively seek the case against a claim, not just the case for it (the adversarial cross-check).
In this suite
The six-stage pipeline references/method.md — decompose → fan out → triage → cross-verify → synthesize → capture.
