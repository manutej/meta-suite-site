the physics of light
What's happening · light obeys laws
radiate INVERSE-SQUARE

Light spreads from the source and dims with the square of the distance — twice as far, a quarter as bright.

occlude RAY-CAST

Anything solid blocks the straight rays. The shadow is just the shape of what the light can't reach.

soften UMBRA · PENUMBRA

Give the source real size and the edge blurs — a dark core ringed by a lighter fringe.

Penumbra = the fringe where the source is only partly blocked, so some light still lands. A bigger light, or a farther surface, widens it.
