A05 · first principles

The physics of light

Not a lighting effect — a real 2D light engine. The shadows are computed: every occluder's edges are projected away from the source to find exactly what the light can't reach. The machinery the suite keeps under the hood, on a concrete job: writing a launch post. Every step of the work gets exactly one instruction block, the blocks come in the same order as the steps, and — the test that matters — add a step to the work and a matching block appears in the prompt, in the right place.
